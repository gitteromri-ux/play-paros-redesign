Just one minute from Paros port and steps from the island's hottest spots, PLAY Paros places you exactly where you want to be — with Parikia's beaches, tavernas, and nightlife as your extended living room.

Paros Accommodations

Brown Hotels／PLAY Paros Hotel／Rooms

Settle into the easy comfort of PLAY Paros Hotel, where laid-back island living meets thoughtful design. Each room is your own private retreat, designed for switching off, slowing down, and enjoying the moment. Walk-in showers, carefully chosen amenities, and a calming atmosphere turn everyday stays into something special.

All rooms share: air-conditioning, Nespresso machine, kettle, mini fridge, smart TV, safe, hair dryer, walk-in shower & free Wi-Fi.
